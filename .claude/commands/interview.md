# Prepare for an interview

Establish the active profile per `CLAUDE.md`. Read its candidate, behavioral,
evaluation and `profile/07-interview-prep.md` references. Use
`python3 tools/application_state.py --profile <slug> list` to select the application;
ask if the company/role matches multiple attempts. Confirm stage, format and date
when they affect the preparation. Development work never selects a candidate.

1. Read the selected application's archived posting, submitted CV/letter and
   `outcome.md`. Use the tracker's `archive` path, not a newly guessed company slug.
   An absent archive or document is a gap to disclose, not permission to invent
   what the employer received. For an untracked interview, ask for the posting
   and submitted materials before making claims about them.
2. Research the employer through its official sources following
   `../skills/job-application-assistant/09-web-research.md`. Verify each company
   fact included in the pack and attach its source. Interviewer research uses
   only public professional information relevant to this interview.
3. Build a stage-specific pack: likely questions from the posting and previous
   feedback; STAR examples from the profile; honest bridge answers for gaps;
   claims in the submitted documents the interviewer may probe; questions to ask;
   and confirmed logistics. Do not invent experience or upgrade ownership.
4. Save `interview_prep_<stage>.md` under this application's archive. Sanitize the
   stage into one filename component and keep all output in the selected profile.
   Present key preparation points and disclose missing source material.
5. Offer a mock interview in this conversation. Ask one question at a time and
   give concrete feedback grounded in the profile and posting. Proposed new STAR
   stories require the candidate's confirmation before entering their profile.

Use `/outcome` to record feedback and the next stage after the real interview.
